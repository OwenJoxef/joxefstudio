Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data I collect when you contact me, book me or visit this website, why I need it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible for your data

Owen Joxef, trading as Joxef Studio, Södermalm, Stockholm, Sweden, is the data controller. For anything to do with your personal data, email Dondjbookings@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Enquiries. When you use the contact form or email me: your name, email address, the service you are interested in, a preferred date and anything you write in your message.
  • Bookings, sessions and lessons. The details needed to deliver and invoice them, such as name, company name, billing address, event details, and payment references from Swish or Bankgiro. If you buy a lesson pack as a gift, the recipient's name if you give it to me.
  • Website visits. This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and all fonts and images are served from the site itself. The hosting provider, GitHub, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

You need to give your name and email address for me to reply to an enquiry. Everything else is optional.

Why I use it, and the legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote, at your request before we agree a booking (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To deliver a DJ booking, studio session or lesson that we have agreed (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To invoice and keep accounting records, as required by the Swedish Accounting Act, Bokföringslagen (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • To keep the website secure, through the hosting provider's security logs (Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest).

I do not use your data for marketing, I do not sell it, and I do not make automated decisions about you.

Who else handles it

I use a small number of service providers to run this website and my inbox:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives what you send through the contact form and forwards it to my email.
  • Google (Gmail) stores my email, including enquiries and replies.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts this website.

These providers may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. Google and GitHub transfer data under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. I only share your data with others where the law requires it, for example with the Swedish Tax Agency.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months of our last contact.
  • Invoices and other accounting records are kept for seven years after the end of the calendar year they relate to, as Bokföringslagen requires.
  • Other booking correspondence is deleted within 24 months of the booking.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data I hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, unless I must keep it by law;
  • restrict or object to how I use your data;
  • receive the data you gave me in a portable format.

Email Dondjbookings@gmail.com to use any of these rights. I will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (imy.se).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
